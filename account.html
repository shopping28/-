About Shopping28

Shopping28 is a modern online earning platform created to provide real and sustainable income opportunities through smart partnership and task-based earning systems.

Our platform offers structured monthly and weekly target earning plans, VIP level bonus rewards, and a powerful partner referral earning system. The main earning method of Shopping28 is inviting and joining partners, where users can build a strong network and earn consistently through team performance.

Shopping28 is not just an earning platform — it is a growing digital ecosystem focused on transparency, innovation, and sustainable income.
